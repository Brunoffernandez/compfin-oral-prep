\subsection{The task (in my own words)}\label{the-task-in-my-own-words}}

\textbf{Exercise 1 --- LSM (Longstaff--Schwartz, 2001).} - Part (i):
reproduce the textbook 8-path numerical example from the paper. Strike
\texttt{K\ =\ 1.10}, \texttt{r\ =\ 6\%}, three yearly exercise dates
(\texttt{dt\ =\ 1}, \texttt{T\ =\ 3}). Print the cash-flow matrix at
each time, the in-the-money regression tables, the fitted
\texttt{E{[}Y\textbar{}X{]}\ =\ a0\ +\ a1\ X\ +\ a2\ X\^{}2}, the
optimal early-exercise decisions, the stopping rule, the option
cash-flow matrix, and finally the American and European put prices to 5
decimals. - Part (ii): replace the 8 hard-coded paths with
\texttt{N\ =\ 100,000} simulated GBM paths (50k + 50k antithetic),

replicate the ``red box'' columns of Table 1 of the paper (Simulated
American, s.e., closed-form European, early-exercise value) for the 20
\texttt{(S0,\ sigma,\ T)} combinations. Here the basis is the first
three weighted Laguerre polynomials evaluated at \texttt{x\ =\ S/K}.

\textbf{Exercise 2 --- Finite Difference (FTCS).} - Part (i): adapt a
provided \texttt{ftcs\_bs\_put.py} to price European puts via an
explicit (forward-time, central-space) scheme in log-price space
\texttt{m\ =\ log\ S}, for the same 20 parameter combinations, and
compare against closed-form Black--Scholes. - Part (ii): turn the
European FTCS solver into an American one by adding the early-exercise
(free-boundary) projection \texttt{U\ =\ max(U,\ intrinsic)} after each
time step. Compare FD-American against the LSM-American of Exercise 1 as
a correctness check. - Part (iii): assemble the full ``outside the red
box'' columns of Table 1 (FD American, closed-form European, FD
early-exercise value, and the FD-minus-LSM difference in early-exercise
value).

\subsection{The hardest question Fang could ask here --- and my

\textbf{Likely hardest: ``Your FTCS scheme is explicit. Is it stable for
\texttt{Nx\ =\ 1000}, \texttt{Nt\ =\ 40000*T}? Derive the condition and
check it for your worst-case \texttt{sigma\ =\ 0.4}.''}

Factual setup from the code: in log-space the diffusion coefficient is
\texttt{0.5*sigma\^{}2}, \texttt{h\ =\ L/Nx} with
\texttt{L\ =\ 2\ log\ K\ +\ 2\ =\ 2\ log\ 40\ +\ 2\ ≈\ 9.376}, so
\texttt{h\ ≈\ 0.00938}; \texttt{k\ =\ T/Nt\ =\ 1/40000\ =\ 2.5e-5} per
unit time.

The explicit-scheme stability constraint for the diffusion part is
roughly \texttt{0.5*sigma\^{}2\ *\ k\ /\ h\^{}2\ \textless{}=\ 1/2},
i.e.~\texttt{k\ \textless{}=\ h\^{}2\ /\ sigma\^{}2}.
